⓿❹ A Team of Unignorable People ⓿❹

Lisa Sutton Gardner – Founder & CEO

Lisa founded Enigma over thirty years ago in the hardworking city of Newcastle (that’s right she was only twelve at the time). She successfully scaled into Sydney over a decade ago, and now oversees over 80 people in creative and media across the only independent agency in this market with both a metro and a regional footprint. She’s as passionate as ever about the power of creativity to drive commercial competitive advantage.

Simon Lee – Chief Creative Officer & Partner

A nomadic youth and a degree in French and philosophy fuelled Simon’s insatiable passion for ideas and the impact they can have on the world. He’s one of Australia’s most accomplished creative leaders and is lit up by the alchemy of turning complexity into simply compelling creative.

Kate Shepherd – Managing Director

In a past life, Kate lived in NYC and was a Vogue travel features writer. Today she paces the corridors of Enigma in her designer wardrobe, balancing creative passion and commercial astuteness, ensuring smooth delivery and campaign results that are truly unignorable.

Aldo Ferretto – Creative Director & Head of Art

Chilean born, Sydney based, Aldo has worked with clients and creatives the globe, delivering campaigns built on a simple belief: different wins. His work has picked up recognition at all major international ad festivals. Trained in Design and Art History, he brings that same craft and care to every brand he builds.

Dom Hickey – Chief Strategy Officer

A painter and crime scene photographer turned world class strategist and leader, Dom is the visionary and driving force behind Enigma’s Central Intelligence department.

Jack Mason – Managing Director, Newcastle

Growing up in regional NSW meant Jack learnt to roll up his sleeves early. After building his career across Australia and the UK, he’s spent more than 18 years helping leading brands grow through marketing, creativity and customer insight. Today, he leads Enigma’s Newcastle team, passionate about building great partnerships, solving business problems and creating work that delivers real commercial impact.

James Leatherbarrow – Chief Financial Officer

James is the fastest member of our leadership team – literally. As well as having a financial mind like a steel trap, he is Enigma’s reigning go-kart champion.

Katie Hope – Head of Production

A painter and crime scene photographer turned world class strategist and leader, Dom is the visionary and driving force behind Enigma’s Central Intelligence department.

Oliver Bates – Head of Operations and Director of IT

If it plugs in, logs in or needs a better process, it probably lands with Oliver. After leading operations and customer experience for digital product businesses, he now keeps Enigma’s creative and media engine moving by connecting people, technology and process so great ideas can move faster.

The Challenge

Equipment hire is about as functional as categories get. Everyone rents the same gear, at roughly the same price, for roughly the same job. Across six years and every kind of customer, from the weekend DIYer to the biggest developers in Australia and New Zealand, one thing kept surfacing: the feeling that comes when Kennards makes the job easy.

The Unignorable Truth

The most valuable thing in the category is the things people are already saying.

The Challenge

We took a much-loved piece of Aussie vernacular and made it Kennards’ own. “Ken” stopped being a turn of phrase and became a whole tribe. A character that was built on a world around the people who say ‘ken oath’ when the jobs been sorted.

Then we let that run wild in the world. One idea carried through from the biggest brand films down to the smallest print and digital executions. The same feeling whether you’re a DIYer hiring a sander or a big-time developer hiring an entire fleet.

The Results

“Ken” earned its place in culture and in the category.

23% Increase in average
value per customer
37% Increase in revenue

We gave Kennards more than a campaign by making the ‘Ken Oath’ language ownable in culture.
